# Procedure 08: Complaints
> Fictional procedure (synthetic data).

## Principles
- Acknowledgement of receipt within 24 h.
- Final reply within 5 working days.
- Tone: acknowledge the problem, apologise sincerely, never blame the customer.

## Common cases
- **Late support reply**: apologise and give a precise date.
- **Customer unhappy with a refusal that follows the rules**: explain the rule with empathy, without changing it.

## To escalate (always)
- Legal threat, lawyer, report, announced public review
- Request for a goodwill gesture or an exception
- Complaint lasting more than 14 days
- Very angry customer or one who insults
