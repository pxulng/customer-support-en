# Procedure 04: Payment and invoicing
> Fictional procedure (synthetic data).

## Payment methods
- Bank card, PayPal, payment in 3 instalments at no cost (A) from €100
- Payment is debited when the order is confirmed.

## Invoice
- Sent by email at shipping and available in "My account > My orders".
- An invoice can be resent on request, without condition.
- Change of billing name or address: possible only before shipping.

## Common cases
- **Payment declined**: check the card details, offer another payment method.
- **Double charge**: one of the two amounts is a temporary authorisation, released within 5 working days. Check first.
- **Promo code that does not work**: check the validity date and the minimum purchase.

## To escalate
- Double charge confirmed after 5 working days
- Suspected fraud or unauthorised payment
- Request for an invoice with a specific professional VAT
