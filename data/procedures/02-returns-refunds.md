# Procedure 02: Returns and refunds
> Fictional procedure (synthetic data).

## Conditions
- Return period: 30 days after receipt
- Product unworn or unused, with labels and original packaging
- Free return at a pickup point for orders over €50 (A); otherwise €4.90 paid by the customer

## Refund
- Made within 7 working days after receipt of the returned parcel and inspection
- Same payment method as the order
- Exchange possible depending on available stock

## Exclusions
- Opened underwear and swimwear (hygiene)
- Personalised products
- Items sold at more than 50% off: exchange only, no refund

## Common cases
- **Request within the period, conditions met**: send the return label and the steps.
- **Request beyond the 30-day period**: refuse politely and make no exception.
- **Customer who has not received the refund after 7 working days**: check the date the return was received.

## To escalate
- Any request outside the written conditions (goodwill gesture, exception)
- Refund not received beyond 14 days
