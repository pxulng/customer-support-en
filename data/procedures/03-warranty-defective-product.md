# Procedure 03: Warranty and defective product
> Fictional procedure (synthetic data).

## Warranty
- Legal conformity warranty of 2 years from delivery
- The defect must predate the request and not be caused by misuse

## Handling
1. Ask for a description of the defect, photos and the order number.
2. If the defect is confirmed: offer replacement or refund.
3. Free return label for a defective product, whatever the amount.

## Common cases
- **Defect visible on receipt (seam, zip)**: replacement or refund, free label.
- **Normal wear after long use**: explain that it is not covered, without blaming the customer.
- **Defect within 30 days**: treat as a return for a defect (procedure 02 does not apply).

## To escalate
- Doubt about the origin of the defect (use or manufacture)
- Product with a safety risk (helmet, harness, combat-sport equipment)
- Customer who threatens legal action or contests a refusal
