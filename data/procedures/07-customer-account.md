# Procedure 07: Customer account
> Fictional procedure (synthetic data).

## Rules
- Forgotten password: reset link sent by email, valid 24 h.
- Change of email or contact details: possible in "My account".
- Account deletion: on written request, processed within 30 days.

## Common cases
- **Reset email not received**: check the junk folder, then the account address.
- **Address update**: guide the customer in "My account > My addresses".
- **Newsletter unsubscribe**: link at the bottom of every email.

## To escalate
- Suspected account hacking
- Request for access to or export of personal data
- Any exchange containing sensitive data
