# Procedure 01: Delivery and order tracking
> Fictional procedure (synthetic data).

## Times (A)
- Standard delivery: 3 to 5 working days after shipping
- Express delivery: 24 to 48 h
- Shipping: within 48 working hours of the order

## Tracking
- An email with the tracking number is sent at shipping.
- Tracking can be viewed in "My account > My orders".

## To escalate
- Confirmed lost parcel, refund or resend
- Customer who mentions legal action or a report
