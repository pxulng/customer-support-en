# Procedure 06: Product and size questions
> Fictional procedure (synthetic data).

## Sources of information
- The product sheet on the site (characteristics, size guide, composition).
- The advisor answers only from this information.

## Rules
- Sizes: refer to the size guide on the product sheet. Do not guess a customer's size.
- Compatibility and use: answer only if the information is on the product sheet.
- Availability: refer to the product sheet. No promise of restocking.

## Common cases
- **"Is this jacket waterproof?"**: answer with the characteristic from the sheet.
- **"Which size for me?"**: give the guide and advise comparing with a well-fitting garment.

## To escalate
- Technical question absent from the product sheet
- Safety or extreme-use question (helmet, climbing)
- Request for personalised medical or sports advice
